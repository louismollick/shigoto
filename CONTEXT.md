# shigoto

Finds lab and food QA jobs for one candidate and ranks them by how worth applying they are.

## Language

### Postings

**Job**:
One real opening, possibly seen on several sources and merged into a single row.
_Avoid_: Posting, listing, row

**Excluded**:
A job hidden because it no longer passes the city or title filters; kept for its reviews.
_Avoid_: Filtered, deleted

**Closed**:
A job every source has confirmed is no longer accepting applications.
_Avoid_: Expired, removed

### Candidate

**Candidate Profile**:
What the reviewer is told about the candidate: skills, methods, experience, languages and constraints from her CV.
_Avoid_: Resume, bio

**Target Metro**:
Calgary, Toronto, Vancouver or Ottawa, including anywhere within about one hour's drive of the city centre. Closer is better.
_Avoid_: Target city, location

**Pay Floor**:
$55,000 CAD a year of base pay, with full-time hourly pay annualized at 2080 hours. A job whose known pay tops out below it is rejected unless the pay is **Negotiable**.
_Avoid_: Pay preference, pay target

**Negotiable**:
Pay below the Pay Floor that can plausibly be negotiated up: the posting leaves it open ("depending on experience", "commensurate with experience"), or it tops out within 5% of the floor.
_Avoid_: Flexible, DOE

### Review

**Hard Blocker**:
A stated requirement or condition that makes a job not worth applying to, regardless of fit.
_Avoid_: Dealbreaker, disqualifier

**Decision**:
Recommend, Maybe or Reject, derived from the final score.
_Avoid_: Verdict, status

**Off-hours Shift**:
A fixed schedule that is overnight or starts before 6am. Always a hard blocker; rotating schedules are not off-hours.
_Avoid_: Night shift, graveyard

**Casual Role**:
Casual, on-call or part-time work. Always a hard blocker.
_Avoid_: Temp
